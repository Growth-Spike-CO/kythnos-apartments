The Beaches of Kythnos

Home / Guides / The Beaches of Kythnos

Kythnos offers more than thirty beaches scattered around its coastline, each with its own character. Some have sunbeds and tavernas nearby, others require a short hike or boat ride to reach. The water is clear, the crowds are manageable even in August, and you'll find everything from the famous double-sided sandbar at Kolona to quiet coves where you might be the only swimmer.

Northwest Coast: The Island's Icons

Kolona

This is the beach that appears in every Kythnos photograph: a narrow strip of sand connecting the main island to the tiny Agios Loukas islet. You can swim on either side of the sandbar, with the sea lapping at both edges. The setting is extraordinary, and it's worth arriving early in summer if you want a bit of space to yourself.

Fykiada

A short walk or boat ride from Kolona brings you to Fykiada, a stretch of golden sand that remains completely wild. There are no facilities here, just the beach and the water. Bring what you need for the day and expect solitude.

Episkopi

Between Kolona and Merichas, episkopi is one of the organized options on the northwest coast. You'll find sunbeds if you want them, and the beach is easily accessible. The water is calm and clear, and the setting feels less dramatic than Kolona but more practical for a full beach day.

West Coast: Near Merichas

Martinakia

This small beach sits close to Merichas port, making it one of the most convenient if you're staying in the main harbour area. The beach is compact, the water is clean, and you can walk here in minutes from the waterfront tavernas.

Apokrousi

Near Episkopi, apokrousi is one of the largest beaches on Kythnos and consistently ranks among the best in the Aegean. The water is a brilliant blue, the sand is fine, and the beach has enough space to spread out. This is where locals will send you if you ask for their favourite swimming spot.

Northeast Coast: Thermal Waters

Loutra

The beach at Loutra is unique on the island because thermal springs feed warm, mineral-rich water directly into the sea. You can feel the temperature change as you swim near the springs. The village of Loutra has been a spa destination since ancient times, and the beach remains popular with visitors who come for the therapeutic waters.

Naoussa (Aosa)

This small, picturesque cove offers a quieter alternative to the busier beaches. The setting is intimate, the water is calm, and the beach has a local feel. It's a good choice if you prefer a more secluded swim.

East Coast: Family-Friendly Sands

Kanala and Megali Ammos

On the eastern side of the island, kanala sits near the beautiful Panagia Kanala church. The beach here, known as Megali Ammos or Great Sand, is family-friendly with shallow, calm water. The setting is protected, the sand is soft, and the atmosphere is relaxed.

Other Beaches Worth Knowing

Agios Stefanos

A reliable choice for swimming, agios Stefanos offers clear water and a straightforward beach experience. It's not the most dramatic setting on the island, but the water is clean and the access is easy.

Lefkes

Lefkes is another option for those exploring the island's quieter coves. The beach is small, the surroundings are peaceful, and you'll likely share the space with just a handful of other swimmers.

Flampouria

Flampouria rounds out the island's beach offerings with another quiet spot for swimming. Like many of the smaller beaches on Kythnos, it remains unorganized and undeveloped, which is exactly the appeal for visitors looking to escape the crowds.
