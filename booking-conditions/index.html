Booking Conditions

These are our standard booking terms. When you make a reservation, we will confirm the specific details in writing, including deposit amounts and payment schedules.

How to Book

All reservations are made directly with Kythnos Apartments. We do not accept bookings through third-party platforms such as or.

Send us an enquiry using the booking form on our website, by phone, or by email. We will check availability for your preferred dates and send you the total cost. Once you confirm you would like to proceed, a deposit secures your reservation. The remaining balance is due before or on the day you arrive. After we receive your payment, we send detailed check-in instructions and door-to-door directions to your apartment.

Guest Information

All guests must provide valid identification upon arrival. Each property has a maximum occupancy, and this number must be respected throughout your stay. The apartment is for your personal use only. Parties, events, and gatherings beyond the registered guests are not permitted.

Payment Terms

We will confirm the deposit amount and payment deadline when we send your booking confirmation. The balance is payable before or upon arrival, as agreed in your confirmation.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation terms will be outlined in your booking confirmation. These terms may vary depending on the season and the length of your stay.

House Rules

Respect for the property, the neighbours, and the local community is expected. Quiet hours apply during evening and night time. Smoking is not permitted indoors. Pets may be allowed in certain properties with prior arrangement.

The terms outlined here are indicative and standard. Exact deposit amounts, payment schedules, and cancellation policies will be confirmed in writing at the time of booking. These terms are for general guidance only and should be reviewed with a qualified professional if needed.
