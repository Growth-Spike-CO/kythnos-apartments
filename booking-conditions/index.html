Booking Conditions

These are the indicative standard terms that apply to bookings made with Kythnos Apartments. All terms are confirmed in writing at the time of booking, and guests should review them carefully before confirming their reservation. We encourage you to consult with a qualified professional if you have questions about any aspect of these conditions.

Exact deposit amounts, payment schedules, and cancellation terms are confirmed at the time of booking and may vary depending on the property, season, and length of stay.

How to Make a Booking

All reservations are made directly with Kythnos Apartments. We do not accept bookings through third-party platforms. To enquire about availability, you can contact us through the booking form on our website, by phone, or by email. We will respond with confirmation of whether your preferred dates are available and provide you with the total cost for your stay.

Once you decide to proceed, a deposit secures your chosen dates. We will send you written confirmation of your booking along with the agreed payment schedule. The remaining balance is due before your arrival or can be settled when you check in, depending on what we confirm in your booking agreement. After we receive your final payment, we send detailed check-in instructions and door-to-door directions to help you find your accommodation without difficulty.

Payment Terms

Payment is handled directly between you and Kythnos Apartments. We accept bank transfers and other payment methods as confirmed at the time of booking. The deposit amount varies depending on the property, the season, and how far in advance you are booking. This deposit is deducted from the total cost of your stay.

The balance must be paid according to the schedule outlined in your booking confirmation. For most reservations, this means payment is due before your arrival date. In some cases, we may agree that you can settle the balance when you arrive at the property. The exact arrangement will be clear in your written confirmation.

All prices are quoted in euros and include any applicable taxes unless otherwise stated. If there are additional costs such as tourist taxes or utility charges, these will be specified in your booking confirmation so there are no surprises when you arrive.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation terms are confirmed in writing when you make your booking. The policy takes into account factors such as how far in advance you cancel, the time of year, and the specific property you have reserved. We understand that plans can change, and we aim to be as fair as possible while also protecting our ability to rent the property to other guests.

If you need to cancel your reservation, contact us as soon as possible. The sooner you let us know, the better the outcome is likely to be. Cancellations made well in advance typically result in a larger refund than those made close to the arrival date. Your booking confirmation will specify the exact refund amounts based on when you cancel.

In the event of exceptional circumstances, we will do our best to work with you to find a reasonable solution. This might include moving your booking to different dates if the property is available, though we cannot guarantee this option will always be possible.

Guest Requirements

All guests must provide valid identification when they arrive. This is a legal requirement in Greece, and we need to register your details with the local authorities. We will ask for passport or national identity card information for all adults staying at the property.

Each property has a maximum occupancy limit, which is stated clearly in the property description and your booking confirmation. This limit is based on safety regulations, the number of beds, and local laws. You must not exceed this number. If you arrive with more guests than you booked for, we reserve the right to refuse entry or charge an additional fee, depending on whether the property can legally accommodate the extra people.

Children are welcome at our properties, but they must be included in the total guest count. Infants and toddlers count toward the maximum occupancy, so please factor this in when you make your booking.

Property Rules

Our properties are residential homes, not event venues. Parties, gatherings, and events are not permitted. This rule exists to respect our neighbors and to maintain the condition of the homes. If you are planning a celebration, please ask us in advance. In some cases, we may be able to accommodate a small family gathering, but this must be agreed upon before your arrival.

Smoking is not allowed inside any of our properties. If you smoke, please do so outdoors and dispose of cigarette butts properly. Any damage caused by smoking indoors, including odors that require professional cleaning, will be charged to the guest.

Pets are not permitted unless specifically stated in the property description. If you want to bring a pet, ask us before booking. Unauthorized pets may result in additional cleaning fees or the termination of your stay without refund.

Check-In and Check-Out

Check-in times are confirmed when you book. We send detailed instructions a few days before your arrival, including the exact address, directions from the port or airport, and information about collecting keys. If you are arriving outside of standard hours, let us know in advance so we can make appropriate arrangements.

Check-out times are also specified in your booking confirmation. We ask that you leave the property in reasonably clean condition. You do not need to do a deep clean, but please wash dishes, take out rubbish, and leave the property tidy. If you leave the property in an excessively dirty state, we may charge a cleaning fee beyond the standard cleaning that is included in your booking.

Liability and Damage

You are responsible for any damage you cause to the property during your stay. This includes damage caused by your guests or visitors. We conduct an inspection after each stay, and if we find damage beyond normal wear and tear, we will contact you about the cost of repairs or replacement.

We are not liable for any loss, theft, or damage to your personal belongings during your stay. We recommend that you arrange appropriate travel insurance to cover your possessions and any potential trip disruptions.

While we maintain our properties to a high standard, we cannot be held responsible for minor maintenance issues that may arise during your stay. If something breaks or stops working, please let us know immediately so we can arrange a repair. We will do our best to resolve problems quickly, but we cannot offer refunds for minor inconveniences.

Changes to Your Booking

If you need to change your dates after booking, contact us as soon as possible. We will do our best to accommodate changes, but this depends on availability. If we can move your booking to different dates, we may charge an administrative fee. If the new dates have a different rate, you will need to pay the difference or receive a refund of the difference, depending on the circumstances.

We reserve the right to make changes to your booking in exceptional circumstances, such as unforeseen property damage or legal requirements. If we need to move you to a different property, we will offer you accommodation of equal or higher standard. If this is not acceptable to you, we will provide a full refund.
