About this space

The Luxury Central Beach House is a self-contained private home a breath away from the shore, 60 square metres in size. The house has a spacious living room, one bedroom, a modern fully equipped kitchen, one bathroom and two courtyards. It is a through-plan, air-conditioned detached home.

The area

The settlement has a pharmacy, a supermarket, banks, travel agencies, a petrol station, a car rental office and a transport network to Chora, Dryopida, Panagia Kanala and Loutra. It also has places to go out for entertainment, food and drink.

Good to know

The house is air-conditioned. There is unlimited free Wifi throughout the house at no charge.
