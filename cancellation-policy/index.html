Cancellation Policy

Home / Guides / Cancellation Policy

This page presents an indicative cancellation policy template. The specific thresholds, percentages and terms that apply to your booking will be confirmed in writing when you make your reservation. We recommend reviewing any cancellation policy with a qualified lawyer before finalizing your booking.

Understanding Your Cancellation Rights

When you book accommodation directly with us, you receive clear written confirmation of the cancellation terms that apply to your specific reservation. These terms balance the need for flexibility with the practical realities of managing a small property where each booking represents a significant commitment of time and resources.

The policy outlined here describes the standard framework we typically use. Your actual booking confirmation will specify the exact number of days before arrival when each threshold applies, along with the precise percentage of your total booking value that applies to each cancellation period.

Early Cancellation Period

Full Refund Window

If you cancel your booking more than [X days] before your scheduled arrival date, you receive a full refund of any deposit or payment you have made. This early cancellation window recognizes that plans change and gives us sufficient time to offer the accommodation to other guests.

The refund is processed to the original payment method within [X business days] of receiving your written cancellation notice. You need to send your cancellation request by email to the address provided in your booking confirmation. We send you written acknowledgment of the cancellation and confirmation of the refund amount and timing.

How the Timeline Works

The cancellation deadline is calculated from the date of your scheduled arrival, not from the date you make the cancellation request. For example, if your booking confirmation states that free cancellation is available up to 30 days before arrival and you are scheduled to arrive on July 15, you would need to submit your cancellation notice by June 15 to receive a full refund.

We count calendar days rather than business days for all cancellation thresholds. This makes the calculation straightforward regardless of weekends or public holidays.

Intermediate Cancellation Period

Partial Charge Window

Cancellations made between [X days] and [Y days] before your arrival date incur a partial charge. During this period, we retain [Z%] of your total booking value and refund the remaining amount.

This intermediate period reflects the reduced likelihood of rebooking the accommodation at short notice. While we make every effort to fill cancelled dates, the closer we get to the arrival date, the fewer potential guests are searching for accommodation during that specific period.

The partial charge covers administrative costs and compensates for the reduced booking window. The refunded portion is processed within the same timeframe as full refunds, and you receive written confirmation of both the amount retained and the amount refunded.

Late Cancellation Period

Non-Refundable Window

Cancellations made within [Y days] of your scheduled arrival date are non-refundable. The full booking value is retained. This reflects the very limited opportunity to rebook the accommodation and the preparation work already completed for your arrival.

By this stage, we have typically declined other booking requests for your dates, completed pre-arrival cleaning and preparation, and arranged various services. The property is effectively held exclusively for you, making it extremely difficult to mitigate the loss from a late cancellation.

No-Show Policy

If you do not arrive on your scheduled arrival date and have not contacted us to cancel or modify your booking, this is treated as a no-show. The full booking value is charged and no refund is provided.

We understand that unexpected situations can prevent you from traveling. If you know you will be delayed or unable to arrive as planned, contact us as soon as possible. While we cannot offer a refund for no-shows, early communication allows us to adjust arrangements and potentially accommodate a later arrival if your circumstances change.

Force Majeure and Exceptional Circumstances

Case-by-Case Assessment

Situations beyond your reasonable control receive individual consideration. Force majeure events, serious illness, family emergencies and other exceptional circumstances do not automatically override the standard cancellation terms, but we assess each case on its specific facts.

If you need to cancel due to circumstances you believe qualify as force majeure, contact us immediately with documentation supporting your situation. This might include medical certificates, official travel advisories, or evidence of other events that prevented you from traveling.

We review these requests carefully and aim to find a fair solution. Depending on the circumstances and timing, options might include rescheduling your stay, offering a partial refund, or providing a credit toward a future booking. Each decision takes into account both your situation and the impact on our ability to manage the property.

What Force Majeure Covers

Force majeure typically refers to unforeseeable events that prevent you from fulfilling your booking through no fault of your own. Examples include natural disasters affecting travel to Kythnos, government-imposed travel restrictions, or serious medical emergencies requiring hospitalization.

Force majeure does not usually include circumstances that were foreseeable when you made your booking, changes in personal plans, work commitments, or general concerns about traveling. The distinction matters because force majeure claims require documentation and assessment against established legal principles.

Making a Cancellation

Required Steps

To cancel your booking, send an email to the contact address provided in your booking confirmation. Include your booking reference number, the names of all guests, your arrival and departure dates, and a brief statement that you wish to cancel.

We respond within [X hours/days] with written confirmation of your cancellation, the applicable cancellation terms, and details of any refund you will receive. Keep this confirmation for your records.

The cancellation takes effect from the date we receive your email, not from the date you decided to cancel or the date any refund is processed. Send your cancellation notice as soon as you know you need to cancel to ensure you fall within the most favorable cancellation period.

Modifying Rather Than Cancelling

If your travel dates change but you still want to stay with us, contact us before submitting a formal cancellation. We can often modify existing bookings to new dates if the accommodation is available, potentially avoiding cancellation charges entirely.

Modification requests are subject to availability and any difference in pricing between your original dates and the new dates you request. This option works best when you have flexibility in your new travel dates and contact us as early as possible.
