About this space

This studio holiday home sits on a one acre plot, on Naoussa (Aosa) beach, Kythnos. It is a fully air-conditioned studio of 27 square metres, a breath away from the shore. The home is on the ground floor, in a complex of two independent holiday homes. On its veranda there is a pergola and an outdoor dining area for cool, relaxed hours, set against the unmatched beauty of the sea and the picturesque landscape around it.

The area

The home is on Naoussa beach, one of the most beautiful beaches on the island, known for its sand lilies in August, in a bay that is well sheltered from the wind where you can moor your boat. The port (Merichas) is 15 minutes away by car, while Chora, the capital, with shops and places to go out (cafes, restaurants and more) is only 10 minutes away.

Good to know

The home is fully air-conditioned with an air conditioning unit. There is unlimited free Wifi throughout the home. The home has a SMART TV.
