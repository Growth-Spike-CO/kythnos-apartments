Brand Background

Kythnos Apartments is a small, independent collection of self-catering holiday homes on Kythnos in the Western Cyclades, looked after by host Giannis Vrettos. Five holiday homes across Kythnos, each one a simple retreat near the sea. You'll find traditional Cycladic houses in Agia Irini, dryopida, and Merichas, chosen for their calm settings and easy access to beaches. Bookings are taken directly, so guests deal with the people who actually run the homes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a stay?

Send an enquiry through our booking form, by phone or by email. We confirm availability and the total, a deposit secures your dates, and we send check-in details and directions.

Where are the homes located?

All five homes are on Kythnos, across the Aosa, Dryopida and Merichas areas. The island ferry port is Merichas.

Do you charge platform fees?

No. Bookings are made directly with us, so there is no third-party platform commission or service fee.

Who is the host?

What is the weather like?

Kythnos has a mild Mediterranean climate, with a main season from May to October. See our live weather page for the current forecast.
