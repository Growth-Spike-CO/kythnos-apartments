When to visit Kythnos

Kythnos has a mild Mediterranean climate. The main season runs from May to October, with warm, dry summers and a sea that stays comfortable well into autumn. July and August are the hottest and busiest months, while late May, June, September and early October are quieter and still warm enough to swim.

The meltemi, the dry north wind of the Aegean, can pick up in midsummer. On windy days the sheltered beaches on the west and east coasts stay calmer than the exposed north, so it helps to keep a couple of options in mind when you plan a swim.

What to pack

  • Light clothing and swimwear for the day, with a light layer for breezy evenings
  • Sun cream, a hat and sunglasses for the strong summer sun
  • Comfortable shoes for the villages and footpaths, water shoes for the pebbly coves
  • A reusable water bottle and some cash, as ATMs on the island are limited
