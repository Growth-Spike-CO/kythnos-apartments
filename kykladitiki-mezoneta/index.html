About this space

The house has two levels. On the ground floor are the living room and the dining area with a fully equipped kitchen, along with the renovated bathroom. The bedroom is on the upper floor. The upper floor runs through the house with an east-facing balcony and a west-facing veranda for relaxed hours against the beauty of Dryopida. On the ground floor there is also a private courtyard with a small table and chairs. The two levels are connected by an internal wooden staircase. It is just 120 metres on foot from the public parking.

The area

The house is in the heart of the traditional settlement of Dryopida, in the village's picturesque white alleys and close to the central square (Piatsa). Dryopida is one of the most important settlements on Kythnos and sits right in the centre of the island, in a particularly convenient spot, since from here you can set off in any direction across the island. Above all, Dryopida is the hub from which the roads begin for Kanala, Flampouria, Agios Dimitrios and every part of southern Kythnos.

Good to know

The house is air-conditioned with an air conditioning unit. There is unlimited free Wifi throughout the house. The house has a SMART TV.

Guest access

Access is very easy. It is 11 kilometres by paved road from Merichas, the main port, and just 6 kilometres from the island's Chora. The home is only 120 metres from the village's public parking. Cars cannot enter the settlement itself because of the traditional steps and alleys.
