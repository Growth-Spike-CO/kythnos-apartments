Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Kythnos Apartments ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website kythnosapartments.com (the "Website"). This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

Last updated: January 2025

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies remain on your device after you close your browser until they expire or you delete them. Session cookies are deleted automatically when you close your browser.

How We Use Cookies

We use two categories of cookies on our Website:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the Website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions you take, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in, or filling in forms.

Essential cookies we use include:

  • Cookie consent preferences: Records your cookie consent choices so we do not ask you again on subsequent visits
  • Session management: Maintains your session as you navigate through the Website
  • Security: Helps protect against fraudulent activity and ensures secure connections

These cookies do not store any personally identifiable information and you cannot opt out of them. Without these cookies, certain services you have requested cannot be provided.

Optional Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our Website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this information to improve how our Website works.

We may use analytics services such as Google Analytics to:

  • Count the number of visitors to our Website
  • See how visitors move around the Website
  • Understand which pages are most popular
  • Identify technical issues or errors
  • Measure the effectiveness of our marketing efforts

Analytics cookies are only placed on your device if you give us your consent through our cookie banner.

Cookie Banner and Consent

When you first visit our Website, you will see a cookie banner informing you about our use of cookies. The banner provides you with the following options:

  • Accept All: Consents to both essential and optional analytics cookies
  • Reject Optional: Only essential cookies will be used; analytics cookies will be blocked
  • Manage Preferences: Allows you to select which optional cookie categories you wish to accept

Essential cookies will be set regardless of your choice, as they are necessary for the Website to function properly.

Your consent choices are stored in an essential cookie so that we remember your preferences on future visits. This consent cookie typically expires after 12 months, after which you will be asked to renew your preferences.

How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Please note that removing or blocking cookies may impact your user experience and some features of the Website may not function properly.

Viewing and Deleting Cookies

Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete cookies individually or all at once
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third party cookies
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Browser-Specific Instructions

For guidance on managing cookies in your specific browser, please consult the following resources:

  • Google Chrome: Visit the Chrome Help Center and search for "clear cookies"
  • Mozilla Firefox: Visit the Firefox Support page and search for "cookies"
  • Safari: Visit the Safari Support page and search for "manage cookies"
  • Microsoft Edge: Visit the Edge Support page and search for "delete cookies"
  • Opera: Visit the Opera Help Center and search for "cookies"

Alternatively, you can visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org for comprehensive information about cookies and how to manage them across different browsers.

Opting Out of Analytics Cookies

If you have previously accepted analytics cookies but wish to opt out, you can:

  • Use the cookie preference tool on our Website (if available) to change your settings
  • Delete analytics cookies through your browser settings
  • Install browser add-ons such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

third party Cookies

Some cookies on our Website may be set by third party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies, and you should check the relevant third party website for more information.

third party services we may use include:

  • Google Analytics for website analytics
  • Social media platforms if we embed social media content
  • Mapping services for location information

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, our operations, or for other reasons. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

We encourage you to review this Cookie Policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
