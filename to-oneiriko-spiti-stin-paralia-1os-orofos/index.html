The Dream Beach House - 1st Floor

A fully air-conditioned studio on the first floor of a beachfront property in Aosa, steps from the water with a shaded pergola terrace overlooking the sea.

★ 5 (1 reviews)

Home / Accommodations / The Dream Beach House - 1st Floor

About this home

This 38 sq.m air-conditioned studio sits on the first floor of a two-house complex, on a 1,000 sq.m plot at Naousa (Aosa) beach. You can walk to the shoreline in seconds. The terrace has a wooden-roofed pergola that provides thick shade for relaxed hours outdoors, with the sea and the picturesque surroundings as your backdrop.

Good to know

The studio has one bathroom and sleeps two guests in two beds. The space is fully air-conditioned. You have access to the entire first-floor studio, with the shaded pergola terrace for outdoor living. This works well for a couple looking for a simple beachfront base in a quiet area. The studio is part of a small complex of two independent houses on the same plot, right by the sea at Aosa.

How to get here

Ferries to Kythnos arrive at Merichas port on the west coast. From there you will drive to the Aosa area on the northeast side of the island. Exact door-to-door directions will be sent to you after you complete your booking.
