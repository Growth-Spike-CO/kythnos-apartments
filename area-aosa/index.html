Aosa Beach Area Guide

Home / Guides / Aosa Beach Area Guide

A Quiet Corner by the Sea

Aosa sits on the western coast of Kythnos, a residential beach area where the houses come right down to the water. You'll also see it marked as Naoussa on some maps. This is not a resort strip or a village center. It's a collection of homes along a small beach, the kind of place where you hear waves instead of traffic and spend evenings watching the sun drop into the Aegean.

The beach itself is compact and picturesque, with clear water and a pebbly shore. A few houses line the waterfront. That's about it. If you want tavernas and shops within walking distance, look elsewhere. If you want to wake up to sea views and swim before breakfast without seeing another person, this works.

Getting Here from Merichas

Aosa is a short drive from Merichas, the main ferry port where most visitors arrive. The route takes you along the island's western coast. You'll need a car or scooter, as there's no regular bus service to this part of the island. The road is paved and straightforward.

Who This Area Suits

This spot works for people who prioritize peace over convenience. Families with young children appreciate the calm water and the fact that the beach sits right outside the door. Couples looking for a quiet base away from crowds find what they need here. Anyone who wants to be near nightlife or within walking distance of restaurants should stay closer to Merichas or Loutra instead.

Our beachfront homes sit directly on this stretch of coast. You step out onto the sand. The sea is there. That's the main appeal of staying in Aosa.
