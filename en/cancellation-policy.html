Cancellation Policy

Home / Guides / Cancellation Policy

This is an indicative cancellation policy template. The exact terms, including specific day thresholds and percentage charges, will be confirmed in writing when you make your booking. We strongly recommend having any cancellation policy reviewed by a qualified lawyer before finalising your reservation.

Free Cancellation Period

You can cancel your reservation free of charge up to [X days] before your scheduled arrival date. If you cancel during this period, we will refund your deposit in full within [Y business days] of receiving your cancellation notice in writing.

Partial Charge Period

Cancellations made between [X days] and [Z days] before arrival will incur a partial charge of [percentage]% of the total booking value. The remaining amount will be refunded to you within [Y business days].

Late Cancellation Period

If you cancel within [Z days] of your arrival date, the full booking amount is non-refundable. This applies whether you cancel in advance or fail to arrive on your scheduled check-in date.

No-Show Policy

Guests who do not arrive on their scheduled check-in date without prior cancellation notice will be charged the full booking amount. No refund will be issued for no-shows.

How to Cancel

All cancellations must be submitted in writing via email to the address provided in your booking confirmation. Cancellations are effective from the date and time we receive and acknowledge your written notice, not from the date you decide to cancel.

Force Majeure and Exceptional Circumstances

We handle cancellations due to force majeure events on a case-by-case basis. These may include natural disasters, government travel restrictions, serious illness, or other circumstances beyond your reasonable control. Contact us as soon as possible if you believe your situation qualifies, and we will work with you to find a fair solution. Documentation may be required to support your claim.

Changes to Your Booking

If you need to change your arrival or departure dates rather than cancel entirely, contact us directly. We will do our best to accommodate date changes subject to availability, though the standard cancellation policy may apply if we cannot offer suitable alternative dates.

Payment Schedule

The cancellation policy applies to all payments made, including deposits and final balance payments. The specific payment schedule and amounts will be outlined in your booking confirmation.
