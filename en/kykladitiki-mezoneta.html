Dryopida, Kythnos

Cycladic Maisonette

A two-level traditional house in Dryopida with a bedroom, fully equipped kitchen, and private courtyard just 120 metres from public parking.

★ 5 (4 reviews)

Home / Accommodations / Cycladic Maisonette

About this home

This two-level house sits in the village of Dryopida. The ground floor has a living room, dining area with a fully equipped kitchen, and a renovated bathroom. The bedroom is on the upper floor. The upper floor runs through the entire width of the house, with an east-facing balcony and a west-facing veranda where you can spend quiet hours looking out over Dryopida. The ground floor opens to a private courtyard with a table and chairs. An internal wooden staircase connects the two levels.

Good to know

The house accommodates up to three guests across three beds in one bedroom, with a bathroom on the ground floor. The internal wooden staircase means you will move between floors during your stay. The fully equipped kitchen lets you prepare meals, and the private courtyard gives you outdoor space within the property. Dryopida is a traditional village in the interior of Kythnos. The house is 120 metres on foot from the public parking area, so you will walk a short distance with your luggage. The two outdoor spaces, the east balcony and west veranda, face different directions for morning and evening light.

How to get here

Ferries to Kythnos arrive at Merichas port on the west coast. From Merichas, you will drive to Dryopida in the island's interior. Exact directions to the house will be sent to you after booking.
