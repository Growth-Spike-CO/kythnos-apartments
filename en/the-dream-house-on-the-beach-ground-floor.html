The Dream House on the Beach, ground Floor

An air-conditioned studio on the beach at Naousa (Aosa), sleeping 2 guests just steps from the water.

About this home

This 27 sq.m. Studio sits on a 1-acre plot right on Naousa beach in Kythnos. You stay on the ground floor of a small complex with two independent holiday homes, and the sea is only a few steps away. The studio comes with full air conditioning and a veranda with a pergola and outdoor dining table. You can sit outside in the shade and look straight out at the water and the quiet, picturesque coastline that surrounds you.

Good to know

The studio has 2 beds and 1 bathroom, with enough space for 2 guests. The layout is simple and practical: one open-plan room with sleeping area, bathroom, and direct access to the shaded veranda where you can eat or relax outdoors. This ground-floor studio suits couples or solo travellers who want to stay right on the beach in a quiet part of Kythnos. The setting is calm and picturesque, with the sea a few steps from your door and plenty of space around the property.

How to get here

Ferries to Kythnos arrive at Merichas port on the west coast. From Merichas, you drive to the Aosa area on the northern part of the island. Detailed directions to the property will be sent to you after you book.
