Merichas Port: Your Gateway to Kythnos

Home / Guides / Merichas Port: Your Gateway to Kythnos

The Working Heart of Kythnos

Merichas sits on the west coast as the island's main ferry port, where most visitors first set foot on Kythnos. Unlike some Greek island ports that exist solely for arrivals and departures, this one has settled into a comfortable rhythm as a proper coastal village. Ferries dock several times daily, bringing a steady flow of people and provisions, but between arrivals the waterfront returns to its own pace.

The harbor curves around a natural bay, with fishing boats moored alongside the ferry dock. Tavernas line the waterfront, their tables set out under awnings where you can watch the comings and goings over lunch or dinner. A handful of shops sell basics and beach supplies. The atmosphere is practical rather than polished, which suits the island well.

Two Beaches Within Walking Distance

Episkopi beach stretches north of the port, a long sandy strip that remains surprisingly quiet even in summer. The water slopes gently, making it popular with families. A few sunbeds are available, but plenty of space remains for those who bring their own towels.

Martinakia lies just south, a smaller beach tucked into a rocky cove. The setting feels more enclosed, with tamarisk trees providing natural shade. Both beaches are close enough to reach on foot from the port area.

Why Stay Here

Merichas works well as a base for several practical reasons. You can walk off the ferry and reach your accommodation without arranging transfers. The port has enough tavernas and shops that you don't need to travel for every meal or forgotten item. Car and scooter rental offices operate near the harbor, making it easy to collect a vehicle and start exploring.

The location on the west coast puts you within reasonable driving distance of most island destinations. Loutra and its thermal springs are a short trip north. Dryopida and the cave systems lie inland to the southeast. The southern beaches require a longer drive but remain accessible for day trips.

Our home in this area gives you the convenience of port proximity without sacrificing peace. You can walk to the waterfront when you want activity and retreat to quiet surroundings when you don't. The setup suits travelers who value independence and easy access to the rest of the island.
