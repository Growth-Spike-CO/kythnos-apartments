House by the Sea

An air-conditioned Cycladic home for four on a 1,000 sq.m. Plot at Naousa Beach (Aosa), steps from the shore.

★ 5 (13 reviews)

Home / Accommodations / House by the Sea

About this home

This 60 sq.m. Holiday home sits on a 1,000 sq.m. Plot at Naousa Beach (Aosa), Kythnos, within breathing distance of the waterfront. The house has air conditioning throughout, a spacious living room with kitchen, two bedrooms, and one bathroom. Two terraces on different levels give you outdoor space to relax. The elevated terrace has a pergola with a 2plank wooden shade structure that creates dense shadow, perfect for cool hours of daydreaming on the veranda with the unmatched beauty of the sea as your backdrop.

Good to know

The house sleeps four guests across two bedrooms with four beds total, sharing one bathroom. The open-plan living and kitchen area gives you room to gather, while the terraces extend your living space outdoors with direct sea views from the elevated pergola area. This Cycladic home suits couples or a small family looking for a quiet beachside stay in Aosa. The large plot and immediate beach access make it easy to spend your days by the water without needing to go anywhere else.

How to get here

Ferries arrive at Merichas port on the west coast of Kythnos. From there you will drive to the Aosa area on the northeast side of the island. Detailed door-to-door directions will be sent to you after your booking is confirmed.
