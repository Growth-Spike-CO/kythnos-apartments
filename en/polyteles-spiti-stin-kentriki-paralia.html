Luxury Central Beach House

About this home

This 60 square meter house sits steps from the shore in Merihas. The property includes a spacious living room, one bedroom, a modern fully equipped kitchen, one bathroom, and two courtyards. The house is air-conditioned throughout and designed as a detached single-story residence with a cross-ventilated layout. You have complete privacy in your own standalone home by the sea.

Good to know

The house accommodates two guests with two beds in one bedroom. The modern kitchen comes fully equipped, and the bathroom serves the entire property. Both courtyards offer outdoor space for your stay. Merihas is the main port area of Kythnos on the west coast. This Cycladic-style house puts you right on the beachfront with immediate access to the water. The cross-ventilated design and air conditioning keep the space comfortable throughout your visit.
