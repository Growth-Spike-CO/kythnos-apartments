About

Home / About

These five homes belong to one family who grew up spending summers on Kythnos. Each property sits in a different part of the island, from the fishing village of Merichas to the hillside settlement of Dryopida. You can choose a house right on the beach at Agia Irini or a maisonette tucked into the old stone streets. The houses range from studios for two to family homes sleeping four. All have kitchens, private bathrooms, and outdoor spaces where you can sit with morning coffee or evening wine. The style stays simple: whitewashed walls, wooden shutters, and furnishings that suit the Cycladic setting. You'll have direct contact throughout your stay. Questions about the house, the island, or where to swim get answered quickly. The goal is straightforward: give you a comfortable base and let Kythnos do the rest.

Kythnos

Kythnos has two main villages: Chora with its red-tiled roofs perched inland, and the older settlement of Dryopida built into a ravine. Merichas serves as the main port where ferries arrive from Lavrio. The thermal springs at Loutra have drawn visitors since ancient times, and the island counts more than 70 beaches along its coastline. Most remain accessible only by boat or dirt road, which keeps them peaceful.
